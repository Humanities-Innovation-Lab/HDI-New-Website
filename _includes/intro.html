Humanities Data Inquiry

Humanities Data Inquiry (HDI) is a multi-phase interdisciplinary research program examining the digital transformation of humanities research. Established in 2021 and continuing through 2029, the project investigates how humanities researchers and information professionals — including archivists, librarians, curators, technologists, and cultural heritage professionals — design, build, govern, and sustain research and cultural heritage infrastructure in an era of rapidly expanding digital and computational research.

Drawing together ethnographic, historical, comparative, and infrastructure-focused approaches, HDI examines the changing relationships among research practice, data, preservation, governance, ethics, and long-term stewardship within the humanities and cultural heritage ecosystem.
